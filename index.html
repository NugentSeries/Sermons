About Pastor Ron Nugent

Ron Nugent was born in Perth, Western Australia, and grew up in Northam and Perth. He was educated at the University of Western Australia (UWA), where he majored in climatology, and the Secondary Teachers' College of Western Australia (which later merged with UWA).

He later taught for several years in secondary schools in the East Malaysian states of Sarawak and Sabah under the Australian Volunteers Abroad program. It was while teaching in East Malaysia that Ron was converted from atheism to Christianity. It was also while teaching in East Malaysia that Ron met his wife Mina, who is a member of Sarawak's indigenous Kenyah ethnic group.

After marriage, he and Mina studied for three years at the Bible College of South Australia, after which Ron was ordained as a Presbyterian minister and served as the pastor of Calvary Presbyterian Church, an Aboriginal congregation in the wheatbelt town of Brookton, and as pastor of Westminster Presbyterian Church in Bull Creek, a suburb of Perth, where he commenced a ministry to Indonesian students and immigrants.

He later planted All Nations Presbyterian Church, a multi-ethnic congregation in the inner-city of Perth.

Ron also served for many years as the State Adviser to the Overseas Christian Fellowship, a student-led ministry to international students in Australian universities. He has been the speaker at church camps and Christian conferences in every state of Australia (except Victoria) and in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines.

He is now retired from pastoral ministry but continues to preach frequently. Ron and Mina have three children and six grandchildren.
