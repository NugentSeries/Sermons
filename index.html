About Pastor Ron Nugent

Portrait
Coming Soon

Pastor Ron Nugent was born in Perth, Western Australia, and educated at the University of Western Australia, where he majored in climatology, and at the Secondary Teachers' College of Western Australia, where he received the Principal's Prize for Professional Competence.

After graduation he taught in the East Malaysian states of Sarawak and Sabah under the Australian Volunteers Abroad programme. During these years he was converted from atheism to Christianity, and also met his wife, Mina, a member of Sarawak's indigenous Kenyah people.

Following their marriage, Ron and Mina studied for three years at the Bible College of South Australia. Ron was ordained as a Presbyterian minister and served at Calvary Presbyterian Church in Brookton and Westminster Presbyterian Church in Perth before planting All Nations Presbyterian Church, a multicultural congregation in inner-city Perth.

He also served for many years as State Adviser to the Overseas Christian Fellowship, ministering to international students in Australian universities. Pastor Nugent has spoken at church camps and Christian conferences throughout Australia (except Victoria), as well as in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines.

Although now retired from full-time pastoral ministry, he continues an active preaching ministry. Ron and Mina have three children and six grandchildren, all living in Perth.
